## 🌊 About Terminal

Terminal is more than a coding study group.

We believe that meaningful growth happens when people with different backgrounds, skills, and ambitions come together to build something.

Our community welcomes everyone, from complete beginners taking their first steps in programming to developers looking for new challenges.

**Our mission is simple:**

- **Learn** — Explore AI, software development, and emerging technologies.
- **Build** — Turn ideas into real products through side projects.
- **Connect** — Meet people interested in technology, startups, and innovation.
- **Grow** — Develop technical skills, teamwork, and problem-solving abilities.

## 🚀 What We Do

| Activity | Description |
|---|---|
| 🤖 AI & Development | Study AI, web and app development, and modern technologies |
| 💻 Side Projects | Design, build, and deploy real-world applications |

| 🤝 Networking | Connect with students, developers, and startup enthusiasts |
| 📚 Knowledge Sharing | Share technical knowledge, experiences, and learning resources |
| 🌱 Career & Startup | Explore career paths, entrepreneurship, and new opportunities |

## 🌐 Our Vision — Busan 2027

**From Dong-A to Busan, and Beyond.**

Terminal started as a small development study group at Dong-A University.

In 2027, we aim to expand into a broader AI and technology networking community connecting students, developers, and aspiring entrepreneurs across Busan.

Our goal is to create an environment where anyone with curiosity and ambition can find collaborators, explore opportunities, and transform ideas into reality.

We are currently building our core team for this next chapter.
